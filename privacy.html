Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Purpose

OFW Bridge is a privately operated integration that reads authorized OurFamilyWizard calendar information and synchronizes selected parenting-schedule and event details to a Google Calendar designated by the operator. This public website is informational only.

Information processed

The integration may process calendar event titles, dates, times, descriptions, parenting-day assignments, exchange details, and related identifiers needed for synchronization. It uses Google OAuth credentials to access the authorized calendar.

How Google Calendar data is used

With the account holder's authorization, OFW Bridge can read, create, modify, and delete calendar entries required to keep the designated calendar aligned with the source schedule. Its synchronization logic tracks identifiers of entries it manages. The application does not use Google Calendar information for advertising or sell it.

Storage and sharing

Integration configuration, authorization tokens, and synchronization records are stored on privately managed infrastructure rather than on this GitHub Pages website. Calendar information is transferred to Google Calendar to provide the requested synchronization. The application does not intentionally share personal calendar information with unrelated third parties.

Retention and control

Synchronization metadata and authorization credentials are retained while needed to operate the integration. The operator can disable synchronization, remove stored credentials or data, and delete the integration's managed events. Revoking Google's authorization prevents further authorized API access, though previously synchronized events may remain in Google Calendar until removed.

Revoking access

The account holder can revoke OFW Bridge's Google account access from Google Account connections. Access may also be disabled on the host running the integration.
