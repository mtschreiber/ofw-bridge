OFW Bridge

A privately operated utility for synchronizing parenting-schedule information and calendar events from OurFamilyWizard with a designated Google Calendar.

About this integration

OFW Bridge helps the operator maintain a calendar view of parenting days, scheduled exchanges, and relevant events. It uses authorized access to Google Calendar to create, update, and remove managed calendar events as schedules change.

This informational website does not connect to, expose, or provide access to the operating system, private calendars, or parenting information.

Access and privacy

Google account access requires the account holder's consent. Learn more about the integration's data handling and revocation options in the privacy policy.
